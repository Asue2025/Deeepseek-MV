# 红昭愿 · painted lyric film (173 s)

**Logline.** A young woodcarver meets a rider by the bridge under the pagoda. He leaves for the frontier and never returns; his horse comes home alone. Through a lifetime of carving, embroidery and tea she keeps his face, and at last lets the red veil go into the spring wind — he stays in her heart.

**Song grid.** 115 BPM, 4/4, bar 2.08696 s, first downbeat .048 s. Lyric starts refined from the LRC against the vocal onsets. Video 24 fps, 173.22 s, 1920×1080.

**World.** One Jiangnan water-town: white walls, slate roofs, a stone arch bridge, a tall pagoda, willows and blossom. The colour arc runs spring peach/green → desert ochre/red → winter blue/white → amber tea room → rose-gold dawn, and ends on the paper of the opening.

**Motifs.** The carved wooden flower (carved at the start, worn in her hair, on the table at the end); the red veil (given by him, mended, released); tea (two cups — one for the absent); the round moon window; the bridge and pagoda.

**Her arc.** Curious and in love → shy → proud → bereft → patient → serene → free. He is young, bright and on a horse in memory; his empty saddle is the turn.
